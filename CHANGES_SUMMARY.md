## 🎯 Design System Consistency

### Colors
- Primary Blue: `#0061b0`
- Hover Blue: `#0078d4`
- Background: `#000000` (Black)
- Text: `#ffffff` (White)

### Border Styles
- Design cards: `2px solid #0061b0`
- Hover: `border-[#0078d4]`
- Rounded corners: `15px - 20px`

### Shadow Effects
- Blue glow: `shadow-[0_20px_60px_rgba(0,97,176,0.4)]`
- Subtle shadow: `shadow-[0_10px_30px_rgba(0,97,176,0.2)]`
- Enhanced shadow: `shadow-[0_15px_40px_rgba(0,97,176,0.3)]`

### Animations
- Hover scale: `scale-105` or `scale-110`
- Rotation: Cards tilt between `-15°` to `+15°`
- Transition: `duration-300` to `duration-500`
- Smooth easing for all transitions
